# Demo proof photos

`sink-before.jpg` (check-in) and `sink-after.jpg` (after) are crops of
[Before and after under sink.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Before_and_after_under_sink.jpg)
by Robertosplumbing, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The crops remove the "Before" and "After" captions and are shared under the same license.

The SVG and PNG files here are our own illustrations. These photos carry no EXIF capture time, so the
app falls back to the upload time and tells the Verifier the time is unverified.
